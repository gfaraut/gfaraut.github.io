## Biosktech
Since 2022, I am full professor at [LURPA](https://www.lurpa.ens-paris-saclay.fr), and the head of the Research theme Industry 5.0 that focuses on Safety and performances of Human-Cyber-Physical Systems (H-CPS). I am specialized in Control Theory and Behavior Identification at [ENS Paris-Saclay](https://www.ens-paris-saclay.fr), part of the [University of Paris-Saclay](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/). With a strong foundation in Computer Science and Electrical Engineering, I hold a PhD from INSA Lyon and an HDR from the University of Paris-Saclay. My research focuses on Discrete Event Systems, Digital Twins, and behavior identification of complex systems, with applications on intelligent manufacturing or healthcare and Ambient Assisted Living (AAL) modeling. I am actively involved in various professional activities, including editorial roles and technical committees within the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society.

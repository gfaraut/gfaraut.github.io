Curriculum Vitae#


Gregory Faraut#

Professor
Control Theory and Behavior Identification
ENS Paris-Saclay – University of Paris-Saclay
Automated Production Research Laboratory (LURPA)

Address: 4 av. des Sciences, 91190 Gif-sur-Yvette, France
Email: gregory.faraut+pages@ens-paris-saclay.fr

Education#

  • 2022: HDR (Habilitation to Lead Research) in Computer Science, University of Paris-Saclay, France

  • 2010: PhD, Computer Science and Electrical Engineering, INSA Lyon, France

  • 2006: MS, Computer Engineering and Electrical Engineering, Nice Sophia Antipolis University, France

  • 2004: BS, Electrical Engineering and Signal Processing, Nice Sophia Antipolis University, France

Positions#

  • 2022 - Present: Full Professor, ENS Paris-Saclay, LURPA, Gif-sur-Yvette, France

  • 2011 - 2022: Associate Professor, ENS Cachan, LURPA, Cachan, France

  • 2009 - 2011: Assistant Professor, AMPERE Lab, INSA Lyon, France

  • 2008 - 2009: Research Scholar, University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, MI, USA

Research Interests#

  • Synthesis of discrete controllers for Discrete Event Systems (DES)

  • Digital Twin & Behavioral Equivalence

  • Behavior Identification of Complex Systems

  • Healthcare Systems & Ambient Assisted Living (AAL) Modeling

Professional Activities#

  • Member of the International Program Committee (IPC) of Workshop on Discrete Event Systems (WODES)

  • Associate Editor, IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering (T-ASE)

  • Co-chair of Technical Committee “Automation in Healthcare Management” (TC AHM), IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (IEEE RAS)

  • Associate Editor, IEEE International Conference on Automation Science and Engineering (CASE) and International Conference on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics (SMC)

  • Guest Editor (with TC AHM co-chairs), Special Issue on Automation Science and Engineering for Smart and Interconnected Healthcare Delivery Systems

  • Elected member of CNU’61 (National Council of Universities): Computer Science, Signal Processing, Control and Manufacturing systems

  • National Organizing Committee, 12th IFAC Workshop on Discrete Event Systems (WODES’14), Cachan, France, May 2014
